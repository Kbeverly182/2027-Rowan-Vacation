Pick Your Lodging

Choose your preferences below to help filter lodging options that will fit your desires. As you select the attributes, certain lodging options will disappear from the page. Unclicking the attribute brings them back. If you don't care either way on any of the attributes, you don't have to click them at all and everything will be an option. Eventually you should be left with a list of accommodations that you'd be happy to stay at. Then label your top 3 (if there are 3 remaining) with a 1 for your favorite, and hit the submit button at the bottom. Results will be anonymously collected to help reach a consensus. Have fun!

Notes

  • If staying on one of the islands/cayes, a flight/boat is needed to/from lodging as well as back to the mainland for jungle excursions, etc.
    • Flights run every hour and take about 20 min and cost around $88 each way. Includes 1 carry-on and 1 checked bag.
    • Boats are ~1.5 hours, roughly half the price, but sometimes experience choppy seas.
    • Hotels can typically help set these up.
